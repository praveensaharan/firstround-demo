Why it has to be a face

A form gets copy-pasted CV bullets. A face gets an interview. Candidates who speak to a person — even a synthetic one — answer in their own words, under real time pressure, the way they will in the round that follows. The avatar's reaction to a thin answer is information the candidate can read and respond to. Strip the face out and you are left with a questionnaire that tells you nothing a résumé did not.
